Grea Coffee Nations

7 people tipped this

A roastery café tucked into Yeniköy with a courtyard out back. Terrazzo floors, good chairs, good light. They have a big old roaster and supply a lot of the hip cafés in Istanbul with coffee — so you're drinking it at the source.

Good place to sit with a stroller and chill. The courtyard means you're not crammed inside. The cats are around and fully unbothered.

The kind of spot you find on a morning walk along the Bosphorus and end up staying longer than planned. Which is the best kind of spot.
