La Pignata

Nothing fancy. Just good pizza for good prices.

AddressAv. du Mail 3, 1205 Geneva
NeighbourhoodPlainpalais
Best forPizza, quick dinner, takeaway
Tip by@dave

All Italian guys working there, a proper wood-fired oven, and they just crank out good pies at a price that won't make you wince. A margherita is somewhere under CHF 15. In Geneva that's remarkable. This isn't going to blow your mind — it's not that kind of place. But if you want a reliably good pizza, La Pignata will do it every time.

My ritual is to get there, order, and have a small beer while I wait for the pizza to cook. The timing works out perfectly — it's almost exactly the right amount of time to drink that little beer.

"A good margherita for under CHF 15. In Geneva that counts as a miracle."

The tip
  • Margherita, under CHF 15 — the benchmark by which Geneva pizza should be judged
  • Order a small beer when you arrive. It takes exactly as long to drink as the pizza takes to cook.
  • Takeaway works well — they know what they're doing with the box
