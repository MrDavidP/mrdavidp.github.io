Cinema Riviera

Sitting outside on a summer evening with a drink, watching a film under the sky. There are worse ways to spend a night in Athens.
Through the curtains. Exarchia. Summer.
AddressValtetsiou 46, Athens 106 81
NeighbourhoodExarchia
SeasonSummer — open air
Tip by@dave

Going to see a movie might not be top of your list while you're on holiday. But there's something about sitting outdoors, having a drink, a cigarette if you want, and watching a film on a summer evening that is very, very enjoyable. Cinema Riviera in Exarchia does exactly that.

Wooden chairs, trees overhead, the screen lit up against the Athens night. The programming tends toward classics and retrospectives. We caught the Godfather. They had the Greek poster. You get a beer, you sit down, it starts.

The neighbourhood

Exarchia used to be the wild anarchist neighbourhood where you could sip fancy cocktails while actual molotov cocktails hurtled past outside as anarchists clashed with riot police. I'm not sure it's still like that — I haven't been in a few years — but either way it's a good area to check out.

There's also a Saturday market up there worth going to. Not a performative farmers market with nine dollar heirloom tomatoes — it's where real people go shopping. Good produce, good prices, a genuine feel for neighbourhood life in Athens that you don't get on Ermou Street.

The tip
  • Open air — summer only. Worth planning a summer evening around it.
  • Get there a bit early, grab a drink, get a good seat before it fills up
  • Check what's playing — tends toward classics and Greek favourites
  • Saturday market nearby — good reason to spend a morning in Exarchia before coming back in the evening
  • The neighbourhood is interesting to walk around — different register to central tourist Athens
