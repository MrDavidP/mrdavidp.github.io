## GENEVA

### Plainpalais

**Bongo Joe's** ✅ BUILT
- Category: Bar
- Address: Quai Ernest-Ansermet, 1205 Geneva
- Copy: "A record shop that became Geneva's best outdoor bar. Sit outside on the pontoon when the sun is out, order a bottle of cold local white — they only do Geneva wines — ask for the nuts, and watch the swans on the Rhône. They do good crispy pilsners and spritzes too but the white wine in the sun is the move. One of the only places in this city that feels genuinely relaxed."
- Order: Cold local white. Ask for the nuts.
- Season: Spring & summer — outside only
- Page: tip-bongojoe.html

**Tanuki**
- Category: Sushi / Takeaway
- Address: Rue Vignier 5, 1205 Geneva
- Copy: "An older Japanese couple running a tiny takeaway sushi spot. He makes the sushi, she runs the orders. No tables, no dine-in — just some of the freshest sushi in Geneva out of a small kitchen. Once they're out of something that's it for the day. The move: get the large platter, stop at Uchitomi (the Japanese grocery nearby) for a bottle of sake, go home. Better than any restaurant and about the same price. That platter in the photo ran about 110 CHF — honest money for what it is."

### Nations

**Empanadas Factory**
- Category: Restaurant
- Address: Rue de Montbrillant 82, 1202 Geneva
- Copy: "If you work near the UN you already know the problem — there is almost nothing good to eat in this neighbourhood unless you're a senior diplomat with two hours and an expense account. Empanadas Factory is one of the exceptions. Grab empanadas on the way out if you're in a rush. But if you have 25 francs and want to sit down and eat something properly, get the Beef Boliviano — steak and egg on rice with fries, the whole thing. The real move is the sauce picante, a little orange salsa they make in house. You have to ask for it. Don't forget to ask for it. Décor is full Saved by the Bell. It works."

### Cornavin

**Mulligans Irish Pub**
- Category: Bar
- Address: Rue De-Grenus 14, 1201 Geneva
- Copy: "Geneva's best pint of Guinness. Creamy, well-poured, the real thing. Get a bag of salt and vinegar crisps with it. Inside is classic cozy Irish pub. Outside is something Geneva doesn't usually offer — actual urban energy. It sits across from Manor, the big department store, on a street with enough foot traffic that you feel like you're in a proper city for an hour. Good people watching. And if you need to grab groceries on the way home, Manor is right there so the cheeky pint pays for itself in efficiency."

## VAUD

### Lavaux

**Domaine Saint-Amour** ✅ BUILT
- Category: Wine
- Address: Chemin de Baussan 44, 1091 Bourg-en-Lavaux
- Copy: "Lavaux is one of the most beautiful wine regions in the world — UNESCO-listed, steep terraced vineyards dropping down to Lake Geneva with the French Alps across the water. The problem is knowing where to go. Go here. A little hut in the vines with a fridge full of cold rosé they grow on site. Honour system — leave cash in the box, take a bottle, sit down in the vineyard. The train drops you nearby so no car needed. An A+ way to spend an afternoon."
- Getting there: Train to Cully or Épesses
- Season: Spring through autumn
- Page: tip-saintamour.html

## BERNER OBERLAND

### Wengen

**Grand Hotel Belvedere**
- Category: Hotel
- Address: Galliweidli 1440E, 3823 Wengen
- Copy: "Recently renovated with the Tyler Brûlé touch — everything well designed, the staff are genuinely cool, and the food is actually good, which in Switzerland cannot be taken for granted. Eat most meals here. The bresaola from wagyu beef they bring on arrival in the lobby while your room is getting ready sets the tone. The cocktail guy spends his summers at Scorpios in Mykonos. The sommelier is Greek, serves incredible Swiss and German wines, and the dinner wine pairing is worth doing. The spa has hot steam, sauna, cold plunge, and an indoor-outdoor pool that sits in the snow in winter. Getting there is part of it — you park in Lauterbrunnen and take a small train up the mountain. That separation from the car, from the valley, from normal life, is its own thing."

## PARIS

### Latin Quarter (5th arrondissement)

**Hôtel Les Rives Oceanik**
- Category: Hotel
- Address: 29 rue Gay-Lussac, 75005 Paris
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/hotelrivesoceanik/
- Price: ~€200/night
- Copy: "Not trying to be the best hotel in Paris, just a good one at a fair price in the right location. Around €200 a night for the 5th, which is honest money. Rooms are small but right — ironwork balcony, Haussmann view, Tintin poster on the wall. Skip the hotel breakfast. Walk five minutes to Jardin du Luxembourg, find a café on the way, get a coffee and a pain au chocolat, do a lap of the gardens. That's your morning sorted."

### Notting Hill

**Ruby Zoë Hotel**
- Category: Hotel
- Address: 146-164 Notting Hill Gate, London W11 3QG
- Price: under £200/night for top floor

Not the Connaught, but solid, well-located, and the room does what it needs to do. Skip the hotel breakfast. There's a good bagel spot and a coffee place a few blocks away.
