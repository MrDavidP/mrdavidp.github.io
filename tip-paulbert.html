Bistrot Paul Bert

A legacy Paris bistro in the 11th. The question of where to eat dinner, answered.

Address 18 Rue Paul Bert, 75011 Paris
Métro Faidherbe-Chaligny (line 8)
Best for Dinner. Steak frites. Bone marrow if that's your thing.
Tip by @dave
Open in Maps

If you're in Paris and you want to go to a proper legacy bistro and you don't want to spend any more mental energy on the question of where to eat dinner — just go to Paul Bert. It's in the 11th, it's been there forever, and it will deliver exactly what it promises.

The steak frites arrives in a copper pan, pink in the middle, pepper sauce pooling around the potatoes. The bone marrow is the kind of starter that makes you feel like you understand France a little better. The wine list is serious without being pretentious. The room is tiled floors, vintage posters, the low hum of people actually enjoying themselves.

"A proper Paris bistro. The steak frites in the copper pan. A bottle of something red. That's the whole evening sorted."

I'm not a big bone marrow person — but watching someone work through the marrow here with a piece of toast and a glass of Rhône, you start to understand the appeal. If fish is more your thing they usually have a filet of sole or something similar on the menu. Either way you're in good hands. This is a place that has been doing one thing very well for a long time.

The neighbourhood itself — the eastern 11th, around Rue Paul Bert and Rue de la Roquette — is good to walk around before or after. Less touristy than the Marais, more neighbourhood feel. It's the Paris that still exists for the people who live there.

The steak frites. Pink in the middle. Pepper sauce. This is why you came.
The tip
  • Book ahead — it fills up, especially on weekends. Reservations by phone or via their website.
  • The steak frites is the move. Pink in the middle, copper pan, pepper sauce. Order it.
  • Bone marrow as a starter if you're into that — it's the real thing here.
  • Fish option is usually a filet de sole or similar — solid if you want something lighter.
  • The wine list skews Rhône and Burgundy. Ask for a recommendation, they know what they're doing.
  • Métro: Faidherbe-Chaligny on line 8. Five minute walk.
  • The neighbourhood is worth exploring — Rue de la Roquette, Rue Keller, the whole eastern 11th.
