Sagano Take Away

The place for the rest of us. Thank you, Sagano.

Address Rue de Montbrillant 86, 1202 Geneva
Neighbourhood Nations
Best for Lunch, park eating
Tip by @dave

Nations is a food desert. The people with good jobs and long expense accounts go next door to Sagano, the legit Japanese restaurant, and sit down for three-hour lunches. This is the place for the rest of us — and it is excellent.

The sushi is made in the same kitchen. Poke bowls, dumplings, and sometimes a chicken katsu over rice if you're lucky. Everything is fresh, properly made, and priced like takeaway rather than like a UN diplomat's lunch budget.

"Made in the legit Japanese restaurant next door. This is the place for the rest of us."

The display case. Rainbow rolls, maki, nigiri. Point and go.

Get whatever's in the case, take it to the park — Parc Vermont is right there, same as the Mérigonde sandwich move. Eat on a bench in the sun with the UN building in the background. Nations suddenly makes a lot more sense as a lunch destination.

The tip
  • Sushi, poke bowls, dumplings — check what's in the case that day
  • Chicken katsu over rice when they have it — get it
  • Take it to Parc Vermont, two minutes walk
  • Entrance is the take away window on the right — not the main Sagano restaurant door
  • Lunch only — go before 1pm if you want the best selection
