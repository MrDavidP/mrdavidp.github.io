Barney Greengrass

14 people tipped this

Nothing shocking here. Sometimes you forget about the simple things. Get a half sour pickle — not the full sour, the half sour — and some smoked fish. Sturgeon if they have it, Nova Scotia salmon if not. That's New York on a plate.

It's been on Amsterdam Avenue since 1908. The room looks like it hasn't changed much. The green stripe plates, the chalk menu on the board, the pickles in the big jar by the counter. That's the point. You're not going for novelty, you're going for the thing that's been right this whole time.

Go for breakfast or a late morning. Order more than you think you need. Take a pickle for the road.

"Half sour, not full sour. Smoked fish. That's New York on a plate and it's been right this whole time."
