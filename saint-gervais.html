Saint-Gervais
The mural above the Manor. Best grocery in central Geneva. Best Guinness. A bar on the Rhône.
City Geneva, Switzerland
Tips 4
Getting there Cornavin station, trams 14 & 18

The neighbourhood around Cornavin station. Busy, central, slightly chaotic in the way stations always make their surroundings. Most people pass through. A few things are worth stopping for.

The Manor food hall is genuinely excellent — the fishmonger gets fera from the lake, the cheese section is serious, and there's usually something on action worth picking up. En Faim for a good lunch. Mulligan's for a Guinness. Bongo Joe on the Rhône if the weather's right.
