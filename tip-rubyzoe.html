Notting Hill, London

Ruby Zoë Hotel

Hotel
5 people tipped this

Book the top floor. The view over the Notting Hill rooftops is worth the small premium and it still comes in under £200 a night — honest money for this part of London. Not the Connaught, but solid, well-located, and the room does what it needs to do.

Skip the hotel breakfast. There's a good bagel spot and a coffee place a few blocks away. The Central line is right there for getting into the city. If you're partial to west London — and you should be — this is the base.
