London Pubs & Bars
Local spots beyond the tourist traps.

The best pubs, wine bars, and drinking spots in London from locals who know where to go. Borough Market to Bankside, proper pubs and hidden gems.

London has more pubs than any city on earth — over 3,500 at last count. The challenge isn't finding a pub, it's finding the right pub. The one where locals drink, where the beer is properly kept, where you can actually have a conversation.

Avoid the gastropub chains and tourist magnets around Westminster and Covent Garden. The best drinking in London happens in neighborhoods where people live and work — Borough Market for food and wine, Bankside for river views, Southwark for proper locals' pubs.

Here's where to drink in London when you want to drink like a local.

Borough & Southwark

The food and drink heart of South London — from centuries-old pubs to modern wine bars.

Pub · Historic
The Anchor Bankside
Bankside, SE1
17th century riverside pub with Thames views. Tourist magnet that's actually worth it.
Pub · Historic
The George Inn
Southwark, SE1
London's last surviving galleried coaching inn. National Trust-owned, utterly unique.
Coffee · Borough Market
Monmouth Coffee
Borough Market, SE1
London's best coffee roaster. Get it at source in Borough Market.

Bankside & Waterloo

River views and proper pubs between London Bridge and the South Bank.

Pub · River Views
The Founders Arms
Bankside, SE1
Best Thames views of any pub in London. Watch the city from the water's edge.
Pub · Art Nouveau
The Blackfriars
Blackfriars, EC4
Stunning Art Nouveau interior. Bronze friezes of jolly monks line the walls.

Wine Bars & Modern Spots

London's evolving drinking scene — natural wine, craft beer, and new takes on old traditions.

Wine Bar · Champagne
Champagne Charlies
Charing Cross, WC2
Proper champagne bar in a Victorian railway arch. Oysters and bubbles.

The Perfect London Pub Crawl

A walking route connecting the best pubs from London Bridge to Blackfriars.

Borough to Blackfriars Walking Route

1. Start at The George Inn — Historic coaching inn, perfect for afternoon pints

2. Walk to Borough Market — Monmouth Coffee for fuel, browse the stalls

3. Continue to The Anchor Bankside — Thames views and river atmosphere

4. Cross Millennium Bridge — Walk past St Paul's to Blackfriars

5. Finish at The Blackfriars — Art Nouveau interior, proper London pub finale

Distance: 2.5 miles walking · Duration: 3-4 hours with stops · Best time: Saturday afternoon
