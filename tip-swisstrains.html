Swiss Train Restaurant Car

14 people tipped this

Don't book first class. Buy a standard ticket and walk straight to the restaurant car. White tablecloth, proper coffee, wine if you want it, a table to yourself with the Alps or the forest going past the window.

Legitimately nicer than first class and you can sit there the whole trip. Order something, open your laptop, watch Switzerland go by. Nobody seems to know this is an option, which is why the car is usually half empty and completely calm.

The station at Cornavin has one of the great old Swiss railway murals on the wall — a hand-painted map of the whole country's train network from another era. Worth stopping to look at on the way through.
