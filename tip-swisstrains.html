Ticket Standard — no upgrade needed
Tip by @dave

Don't book first class. Buy a standard ticket — but don't go to your seat. Walk straight to the restaurant car.

White tablecloth. Alps out the window. Standard ticket.

A white tablecloth table, a waiter, the Alps going past the window. Sit down and stay. What you spend on food and drink will probably come to roughly the same as a first class upgrade — but this is legitimately nicer.

The correct order.

"Share a bottle with a friend or a stranger. Watch Switzerland go by. Nobody seems to know this exists, which is why the car is usually half empty."

Play a game. Watch something. Have a long lunch. A proper dining room moving through one of the most beautiful countries in the world at 200km/h, almost entirely to yourself.

What you do when you have time.
The tip
  • Buy a standard ticket — no upgrade, no first class
  • Board the train, skip your seat, walk to the restaurant car
  • Sit down and stay. You can be there the whole journey.
  • Order coffee, wine, food — the bill roughly equals a first class upgrade anyway
  • The car is usually half empty. Nobody knows this exists.
  • Best on longer routes: Geneva → Zürich is the classic
