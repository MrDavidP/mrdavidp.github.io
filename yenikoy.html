Yeniköy on the Bosphorus

Old money, fresh fish, and a waterfront that makes you feel like you found something most visitors never will.

Read
Location
European shore, 25 min from Taksim by bus
Best season
Autumn — bonito runs, golden light, perfect weather
Go for
Raki & fish, great coffee, the long waterfront walk
Don't miss
The fish truck in October. The ferry at dusk.
The Neighbourhood

Yeniköy sits on the upper Bosphorus where the strait narrows and the water turns slate-green in winter. It's an old neighbourhood — Greek Orthodox churches still stand between the yalıs, the grand waterfront mansions of Istanbul's former elite — and that layered history gives it a texture that the more visited parts of the city have long since lost.

The architecture tells the story. Wooden yalıs painted in faded pastels lean over the water. Ferraris park outside the fish restaurant. A man in white gloves cleans fish on a plastic crate while two cats watch patiently from a nearby wall. The taxi drivers have occupied the same tea stand for thirty years. It is a neighbourhood that has not been curated for visitors, and that is the point.

Most people who visit Istanbul never make it this far up the European shore. The tour buses stop at Sultanahmet. The boutique hotel crowd goes to Balık Pazar and calls it local. Yeniköy requires a bus, a bit of curiosity, and the willingness to arrive somewhere without a plan. In return it gives you raki on the water at dusk, the best coffee on the Bosphorus, and a fish sandwich eaten standing up while the ferry goes by.

Come in autumn if you can. The bonito run through the Bosphorus in October and the fish truck appears on the main road selling çinekop and İstavrit straight from the water. The light goes gold. The cyclists are still out on their long Bosphorus rides. The tea houses fill up. It is the best version of this city, and almost no one knows it.

The neighbourhood in one image — old money and fresh fish, coexisting without irony.

Eat & Drink
Restaurant
Arnavutköy Restaurant
"The holy grail of Yeniköy. A proper raki-balık meyhane right on the water. They bring mezzes, you pick your fish from the display, you drink your raki, you watch the boats pass. Don't skip the salad. If you have kids, ask for the smoke-free cabin on the side."

Need to know
  • Bus from Beşiktaş up the Bosphorus road — 25 minutes
  • Everything is better in autumn. Bonito season: September–November
  • Always get the pistachio at MUÀ
  • At Arnavutköy: order the salad. Eat it.
  • The cats were here first. Respect accordingly.
  • Learn two words: balık (fish) and raki
Nearby hoods
