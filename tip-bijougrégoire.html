Watch Shop
Bijouterie Grégoire
The Geneva watch shop for the NOMOS-curious.
Address Vieille Ville, Geneva
Neighbourhood Vieille Ville · Old Town
Best for Buying a NOMOS, window shopping, wandering
Tip by @paddy

Geneva is a watch town. You probably know this. What's less obvious is where to go if you're not in the market for a Rolex or a Patek — and want something equally serious, more attainable, and frankly more interesting to talk about at dinner.

NOMOS is a German watchmaker from Glashütte — independent, design-led, and deeply respected by people who know about these things. They're not Swiss, which in Geneva is almost a contrarian position, but the watches speak for themselves: clean, precise, beautifully proportioned. No unnecessary complications. Pure function dressed beautifully.

"Geneva is full of watches you can't buy. This is one you can — and it's on a beautiful side street in the old city."

Bijouterie Grégoire is the one store in Geneva that stocks NOMOS on site. Not online, not to order — actually in the cases, actually there. It's located on a side street in the Vieille Ville, the old city, which is also one of the most photogenic parts of Geneva. Even if you're not buying, it's worth the walk through the neighbourhood.

Think of it as watch window shopping with an actual destination. The kind of afternoon that ends with knowing exactly what your next watch will be.

The tip
  • The one Geneva store with NOMOS watches physically on site — not online, not to order
  • Located on a side street in the Vieille Ville — the old city, the most photogenic part of Geneva
  • NOMOS is German, not Swiss — but deeply respected by serious watch people
  • Combine with a walk through the old town — the neighbourhood does the rest
  • Good entry point if you love watches but find the big Swiss maisons intimidating
