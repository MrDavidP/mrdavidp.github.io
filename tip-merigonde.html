Chocolaterie Mérigonde

6 people tipped this

A proper boulangerie a short walk from the UN. The kind of place that's been there forever and doesn't need to explain itself. Get a sandwich — the roast beef is one of the best in the neighbourhood — or a slice of quiche, and take it across the street to Parc Vermont.

Under 10 francs. Sit on the grass, eat in the sun, watch the UN building loom in the background. It's one of the better lunches available in a neighbourhood that doesn't have many.

Good chocolates and bread too if you want to take something back to the office. The CHOCOLAT sign on the wall has excellent energy.
