Food Guide · Istanbul · Turkey

Istanbul
The ultimate food city.

Where to eat in Istanbul from locals who know the city — best Turkish restaurants, street food, and hidden gems across Beyoğlu, Kadıköy, and the historic peninsula.

Istanbul is the greatest food city you've never properly explored. Everyone knows about the döner kebab and Turkish delight, but that's like knowing New York for pizza and bagels — true, but you're missing 99% of the story.

This is a city where every neighborhood has its own culinary identity. Kadıköy on the Asian side for the best fish restaurants, Beyoğlu for modern Turkish cuisine, the Grand Bazaar area for traditional Ottoman dishes that haven't changed in centuries.

Here's where to eat in Istanbul when you want to eat like a local.

Traditional Turkish Cuisine

Classic Ottoman dishes and traditional restaurants that have perfected their craft over generations.

Turkish · Traditional
Hamdi Restaurant
Eminönü, Historic Peninsula
Best lamb kebabs in Istanbul. Views over the Golden Horn from the rooftop.
Ottoman · Historic
Pandeli
Eminönü, Spice Bazaar
Ottoman palace cuisine in 1901 setting. Book ahead, dress up, order the lamb stew.
Regional Turkish · Authentic
Çiya Sofrası
Kadıköy, Asian Side
Regional Turkish dishes from every province. Daily changing menu, everything excellent.

Street Food & Casual Dining

Where locals grab quick, delicious food — from balık ekmek on the waterfront to döner done right.

Street Food · Fish
Balık Ekmek Boats
Eminönü, Galata Bridge
Grilled mackerel sandwich served from boats. Iconic Istanbul street food experience.
Döner · Local Favorite
Dönerci Karadeniz
Beyoğlu, Taksim
Best döner kebab in Beyoğlu. Local workers' lunch spot, no tourists.

Modern Turkish Cuisine

Contemporary takes on Turkish classics and innovative restaurants pushing boundaries.

Modern Turkish · Fine Dining
Mikla
Beyoğlu, Pera
Mehmet Gürs' acclaimed restaurant. Modern Turkish cuisine with Bosphorus views.

Sweets & Traditional Desserts

Turkish delights, baklava, and traditional sweets from the best pastry shops in the city.

Baklava · Traditional
Karaköy Güllüoğlu
Karaköy, Galata
Best baklava in Istanbul since 1949. Pistachio baklava is perfection.
Turkish Delight · Traditional
Koska
Multiple Locations
Traditional Turkish delight maker since 1907. Rose and lemon flavors are classics.

Eating by Neighborhood

Where to eat in Istanbul's distinct neighborhoods — each has its own food culture.

Kadıköy (Asian Side)
Best fish restaurants, local markets, authentic neighborhood feel. Take the ferry for the full experience.
Beyoğlu / Galata
Modern restaurants, wine bars, contemporary Turkish cuisine. Trendy dining scene.
Historic Peninsula
Traditional Ottoman cuisine, tourist spots that are actually good, historic restaurants.
