Hotel Breakfast Kigali

Kigali Marriott

One of the best breakfast buffets in recent memory. Beautiful hotel right in the centre of Kigali.

Address KN 3 Ave, Kigali, Rwanda
Best for Staying. The breakfast. Recovering from a long flight.
Tip by @dave
Open in Maps

Out of all the hotel breakfasts across all the trips, this one has stayed with me. There's something about the room — the double-height windows, the curved staircase, the morning light coming in off the garden — that makes the whole thing feel genuinely special rather than just functional. The buffet itself is extensive, properly fresh, and the Rwandan tea (Sorwathe, grown on the shores of Lake Kivu) is worth knowing about.

The hotel sits right in the centre of Kigali, which is itself worth knowing about: Kigali is a genuinely surprising city — clean, hilly, fast-moving, with a quality of life infrastructure that puts a lot of European cities to shame. The Marriott is a reliable base from which to understand all of this.

"The breakfast alone is worth factoring into your accommodation decision."

The rooms are well-sized, well-designed, and look out over a city that is quietly becoming one of the most interesting places on the continent. The light in the morning is something specific to this latitude — golden, direct, completely different from anything European. You notice it when you wake up.

One specific thing to know: the tea at breakfast is Sorwathe, a Rwandan brand grown on the shores of Lake Kivu. It says "Sorwathe is my cup of tea" on the packet, which is a reasonable claim. Pick up a few boxes before you leave — it's a good thing to bring back.

Sorwathe. Grown on Lake Kivu. Pick up a box before you leave.
The tip
  • The breakfast — allocate time for it. Don't rush it. It's one of the better ones.
  • The Sorwathe tea at breakfast is Rwandan, grown on Lake Kivu. Buy some to take home.
  • Ask for a room with a view of the hills when booking — Kigali's geography is beautiful and worth waking up to.
  • The hotel is central — walking distance from the main streets, easy base for exploring.
  • While you're in Kigali: go to Question Coffee for the best Rwandan coffee, and pick up bags to take home.
