Kunsthalle Bern

A white box cantilevered over the trees above the Aare. Good coffee, good drinks, great building.
The bar. Floating over the Aare. Bern.
AddressHelvetiaplatz 1, 3005 Bern
Best forCoffee, drinks, good building
NeighbourhoodKirchenfeld · Bern
Tip by@dave

The Kunsthalle Bern is a contemporary art space across the river, worth going to for whatever is on. But the reason to know about it regardless of the programme is the café — a white modernist box that cantilevers out over the trees above the Aare, half indoors, half out. A dope spot for a coffee or a drink.

The building itself is the tip. Easy to miss if you walk straight in — step back and look at what's going on structurally. Something about a room suspended in trees above a river valley. Bern doesn't get enough credit.

"A room suspended in the trees above the Aare. Bern doesn't get enough credit."

— Dave
The tip
  • The café is worth going to regardless of what's on — but check what's showing while you're there
  • Step back from the entrance and look at the structure — the cantilever over the trees is the thing
  • Good spot for a coffee or a drink, indoor-outdoor depending on weather
  • Easy to combine with the Bernisches Historisches Museum next door or a walk along the Aare
