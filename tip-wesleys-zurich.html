Three Wesley's in Zürich.
Take the one by the station.

A revamped shipping container at Museumstrasse, behind Hauptbahnhof. Order the chicken katsu. Thank Lee later.

Tip by Lee Avnit
·
Wesley's Kitchen
·
Museumstrasse, 8005 Zürich
·
Behind Hauptbahnhof

Three of these in Zürich. The one to know is at Museumstrasse, just behind Hauptbahnhof — a revamped shipping container with cool graphics on it. You can clock it from the platforms. Quick line, in and out.

Get the chicken katsu over rice. Crisp skin, glossy soy glaze, scallions on top, slaw on the side. Eat it there or take it on the train.

"There are 3 of these places in Zurich. I'd recommend the one by the central train station. It's just a revamped shipping container with cool graphics on it. Get the chicken katsu. Thank me later."

— Lee
