Geneva
24 tips from locals who live there.

The complete insider's guide to Geneva — best restaurants, natural wine bars, and hidden spots you won't find in guidebooks. From someone who actually lives here.

Geneva gets a bad rap as expensive and boring. There's some truth to the expensive part, but boring? Not if you know where to look. This is a city where natural wine bars sit next to ancient watchmakers, where you can swim in a pristine alpine lake in the morning and eat Bolivian empanadas near the UN for lunch.

The key is avoiding the tourist traps around the lake and jet d'eau. The real Geneva is in the neighborhoods — Nations where international staff eat well on modest budgets, Eaux-Vives where locals gather at specialty coffee shops, Carouge where the wine bars stay open late.

Here are 24 tips from someone who lives here and eats out way too much.
