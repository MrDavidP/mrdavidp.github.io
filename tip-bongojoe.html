Bongo Joe

A record shop that became Geneva's best outdoor bar

Address Quai Ernest-Ansermet 3, Geneva
Neighbourhood Plainpalais
Best for Afternoon drinks, doing nothing
Tip by @dave

Sit outside on the Rhône and let the river do its thing. The water here is that improbable turquoise-green that takes a minute to accept as real, and it moves fast and quietly, and there is almost always something on it — ducks, swans, the occasional boat. One of the only places in Geneva that feels genuinely relaxed.

They do good crispy pilsners and spritzes, but the white wine in the sun is peak Bongo. They only sell local wines, all good. Ask for the nuts — they're not on the menu exactly, you just ask.

"One of the only places in this city that feels genuinely relaxed."

That's where it is. Right on the water.

It started as a record shop — still is one, technically — and the outside terrace just became the main event. The music is good. The vibe is unpretentious. Nobody is performing anything. Geneva can feel like a city where everyone is busy being correct about something; Bongo Joe is the exception.

Local white, ice bucket, sunflower. Through the window: the Rhône.
Late afternoon on the water.
The tip
  • Get a table outside right on the water — inside is fine but outside is the point
  • Order the local white. It changes but it's always good
  • Ask for nuts — they have them, just ask
  • Pilsner is solid if wine isn't your thing
  • Afternoon is the move. It gets busy in the evening but the light is better earlier anyway
