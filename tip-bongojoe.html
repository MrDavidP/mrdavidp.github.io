Bongo Joe's

Bar
8 people tipped this

A record shop that became Geneva's best outdoor bar. Sit outside on the pontoon when the sun is out, order a bottle of cold local white — they only do Geneva wines — ask for the nuts, and watch the swans on the Rhône.

They do good crispy pilsners and spritzes too but the white wine in the sun is the move. One of the only places in this city that feels genuinely relaxed.
