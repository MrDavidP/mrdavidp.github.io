The Alpine IssueWengen, Switzerland
Hotel●Wengen●Bernese Oberland

Go to Wengen. Stay at the Belvedere. Stop searching.

A car-free Swiss alpine village reached only by cog railway. A newly renovated grand hotel with an outdoor pool in the snow. The wagyu bresaola will stop you cold.

Sushi · Pâquis · Geneva

The best sushi in Geneva. You are briefly in Tokyo.

Sit at the bar, order the uni if they have it, drink cold sake. The chef has been doing this a long time. It shows.

Raki-balık on the Bosphorus. The holy grail.

Swimming/ Pâquis · Geneva

Bains des Pâquis. Don't skip it because it's easy to find.

Locals swim here in all seasons. The sunset from the jetty, the fondue in winter, the cheap breakfast at 7am. This is the soul of Geneva if Geneva has one.

By Dave
Restaurant/ Hauptbahnhof · Zürich

Three Wesley's in Zürich. Take the one by the station.

Revamped shipping container behind Hauptbahnhof. Order the chicken katsu. Thank Lee later.

Wine/ Lavaux · Vaud

Honour-system rosé in a UNESCO vineyard.

Drop twenty francs in the box, take a bottle, walk down to the lake. Best seat in Lavaux.

Deli/ UES · New York

Skip Katz's. Go to Pastrami Queen.

Est. 1956, kosher, no queue. The sandwich you actually came to New York for.

Jazz/ West Village · New York

A New York jazz club. Go to Smalls.

West Village basement, sixty seats, two sets a night. Since 1994. The one you always meant to get to.

By Dave
Bar/ El Born · Barcelona

House cava, one-litre bottle. Anchovies in oil.

Stand at the bar. Don't order anything fancy. This is what you came for.

By Dave
Travel/ Switzerland

Buy the regular ticket. Upgrade yourself to the restaurant car.

White tablecloth, real silverware, a cold beer at 10am. The best-kept secret in Swiss rail.

By Dave
Market/ Carouge · Geneva

Saturday market in Carouge. Arrive hungry.

The good cheese stall, the rotisserie chicken at Henri, a glass of wine standing up at Paul-Henri's.

By Dave
Hotel/ Zürich

Book Ruby Mimi. Stop looking at hotels online.

Smart design, fair price, walk-everywhere location. The Swiss hotel answer you keep missing.

By Dave
Island/ Dodecanese · Greece

Go to a wedding. Break your ankle. Stay two weeks.

Astypalea is the Greek island that hasn't been found yet. Keep it that way, but go once.

By Dave
Pizza/ Plainpalais · Geneva

A good margherita for under CHF 15.

In Geneva that counts as a miracle. Small beer, margherita, takeaway. Done.

Café/ Eaux-Vives · Geneva

The café that didn't exist in Geneva ten years ago.

Good cortado, great hot chocolate, colourful chairs, relaxed everything. Right across from the Eaux-Vives station.

By Dave
Cinema/ Exarchia · Athens

Cinema Riviera, Exarchia. A film under the sky.

Outdoor screen, a drink in hand, summer evening in Athens. There are worse ways to spend a night.

By Dave
Theatre/ Versoix · Geneva

Versoix has a kids' theatre and you should know about it.

Funky little kid-friendly theatre doing rotating shows in English. The kids loved it. The parents loved it more.
