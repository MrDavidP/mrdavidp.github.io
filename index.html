Bar · Plainpalais · Geneva

A record shop that became
Geneva's best outdoor bar

Sit on the Rhône, order the cold local white, ask for the nuts. One of the only places in this city that feels genuinely relaxed. They only do local wines — all good. The turquoise water moves quietly past and there is almost always a duck or a swan.

Wine · Carouge · Geneva

The best wine in Geneva, maybe the world

Paul-Henri Soler is a French winemaker near the airport who bottles the best wine in Geneva and sells it at the Saturday market for CHF 16. Chalk prices on the bottles, free tastings, wooden barrel to stand at. Say his name at Bombar — you'll pass the cool test.

Seafood · Carouge · Geneva

Breton oysters, every Saturday

A frazzled Frenchman drives fresh oysters from Brittany to the Carouge market. Blue barrel table, cold muscadet, Breton flag above the stall. Not guaranteed every Saturday — but usually there.
