Travel · Switzerland

Get a regular ticket. Upgrade yourself to the restaurant car.

The Swiss train restaurant car is one of the great underused pleasures of living here. White tablecloth, Alps outside, cold beer. Nobody knows about it.

Restaurant · Yeniköy · Istanbul

Raki-balık on the Bosphorus. The holy grail.

Sit outside, order the salad first, drink raki slowly. One of those meals that makes you understand why people love Istanbul.
