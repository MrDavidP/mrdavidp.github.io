Grand Hotel Belvedere

11 people tipped this

Recently renovated with the Tyler Brûlé touch — everything well designed, the staff are genuinely cool, and the food is actually good, which in Switzerland cannot be taken for granted. Eat most meals here.

The bresaola from wagyu beef they bring on arrival in the lobby while your room is getting ready sets the tone. The cocktail guy spends his summers at Scorpios in Mykonos. The sommelier is Greek, serves incredible Swiss and German wines, and the dinner wine pairing is worth doing.

The spa is the move — raw concrete architecture, hot steam, sauna, cold plunge, and an indoor-outdoor pool that sits in the snow in winter. Getting there is part of it: you park in Lauterbrunnen and take a small cog railway up the mountain. That separation from the car, from the valley, from normal life, is its own thing.
