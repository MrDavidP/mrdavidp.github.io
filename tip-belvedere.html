Hotel Spa Alps Wengen

Grand Hotel Belvedere

Stop searching. If you want this kind of trip, just go here.

Address Galliweidli, 3823 Wengen, Switzerland
Best for Hotel, spa, food, cocktails, the Alps
Tip by Dave
Maps

Wengen is a classic Swiss alpine resort town near Interlaken in the Berner Oberland. The thing about getting there is that you park your car in the village below and take a cog railway up. This sounds like an inconvenience but it isn't — it's the beginning of the separation process from real life to actual vacation. The ride is beautiful and just something so perfectly Swiss about the whole thing. Then the hotel sends a small car to collect you at the station, which is exactly the right kind of detail for a place like this.

The ride up. Part of the point.

The hotel was recently renovated and Tyler Brûlé was somehow involved. The taste and attention to detail is unmistakably Brûlé — the bar wallpaper, the lobby furniture, the way everything sits together. It feels considered without being fussy.

We sat in the lobby while our room was being prepared. I ordered a beer and the wagyu bresaola. Say it out loud: wagyu bresaola. Switzerland is not known for its food. It's a bread, cheese, and keep it moving kind of country. Even the best restaurants here don't usually hit that certain spot. My expectations are always low. When that wagyu bresaola hit my tongue I was genuinely in shock. It was so good, and every meal afterwards was excellent. This is a huge thing in the Helvetic Confederation. I would eat all your meals here — maybe one night out for pizza just to see the town — but otherwise, stay.

"My expectations for Swiss food are always low. When that wagyu bresaola hit my tongue, I was in shock."

The sommelier is a friendly Greek guy offering excellent wines from Switzerland and some stuff from Germany that was so good we ordered a case to take home. The cocktail guy is also Greek — he works at Scorpios Mykonos in the summer — and makes an excellent 22 CHF cocktail. Pay the money. Enjoy the cocktail. The smoky Negroni in particular.

I'm not a big ski guy, but this place has everything you need. You just take the little train further up the mountain and can ski all the way back down to Wengen if you want. As for the spa: I'm a huge sauna and steam guy and both were excellent — very hot, clean, proper. Cold plunge, pool, nice showers. My wife, who is a picky customer for these things, gave the massage an A+.

You can absolutely come here, skip the skiing entirely, spa, eat, drink, and leave completely happy.

The outdoor pool. In the snow.
The Tip

Grand Hotel Belvedere, Wengen. Recently renovated, Tyler Brûlé involved. Park your car in Lauterbrunnen and take the train up — the separation from real life is part of the experience. Eat all your meals here. Ask the Greek sommelier what he's excited about. Order the smoky Negroni and pay the 22 CHF without flinching.

You can skip the skiing entirely and still leave completely satisfied. Spa, food, wine, mountains. That's the whole thing.
