Geneva
The city of refuge. Alps in the distance, vineyards, a lake, and rivers.
Tips26
Neighbourhoods11
CountrySwitzerland
LanguageFrench

Home to international organisations, banks, and watches — and the people behind them. The French Alps in the distance, vineyards, a lake, rivers. Switzerland's francophone outpost feels quietly utopian.

Also home to many Portuguese and other Europeans who came to build their lives here, because it's genuinely a lovely place to be. A good place to raise kids.
