Geneva
Country Switzerland
Neighbourhoods 10
Tips 17

The city of refuge. Home to three major things: international organisations, banks, and watches. It attracts the people who work in those things, and also a lot of Portuguese and others who came here to live their lives — because it's a genuinely lovely place to be. The Jura mountains to the west, the French Alps to the east, Lake Geneva and two rivers all around it. Immaculately beautiful in most places, flowing water everywhere that is usually drinkable. You can get a good cortado, a sushi hand roll, some A+ natural wine, a smashburger, and a second-hand Rolex in many windows around the city. The francophone outpost of Switzerland. Clandestine political meetings next to family bicycle rides through the park. Geneva is a wonderful place to have some kids.
