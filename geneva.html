Geneva
Country Switzerland
Neighbourhoods 10
Tips 18

The city of refuge. Home to international organisations, banks, and watches — and the people who work in those things, plus a lot of people who came here to live their lives, because it's genuinely lovely. The Jura mountains to the west, the French Alps to the east, Lake Geneva and two rivers all around it. Immaculately beautiful, flowing water everywhere, and usually drinkable. The francophone outpost of Switzerland. Clandestine political meetings next to family bicycle rides through the park. A good place to have some kids.
