La Barge
A little river bar on the Rhône. Jump in, come back out, drink a cold beer.
Saint-Gervais · Geneva · Switzerland
La Barge
AddressProm. des Lavandières, 1204 Geneva
WhenSummer only — roughly June to September
Best forAfter work, river swimming, cold beer
Tip by@dave

La Barje des Lavandières — known to everyone just as La Barge — is a little pop-up bar right on the Rhône. Colourful furniture, bocce ball, some tables under the trees. Open only in summer. My first instinct was that I didn't like it, because I'm particular about my drinks and plastic cups are normally a hard no for me. But the beers here are shockingly cold and crispy and they just hit. The plastic cups work somehow.

The best part is the swimming. You can literally just step off the bank into the Rhône, stay in for a few minutes, climb back out, and keep going with your friends. I actually prefer river swimming to the lake — the water moves fast enough that it stays genuinely clean, no duck fleas, no still water warmth. This is the best point in the entire city for submerging your body in cold, refreshing water.

"Leave work a bit early. You're at La Barge in 15 minutes. You jump in the water, then drink a cold beer, and you say: this is the greatest city in the world."

La Barge from across the Rhône. Colourful, low-key, exactly what it is.

Further down at Jonction gets chaotic in peak summer hours — too crowded, too much going on. La Barge is calmer. Leave work slightly early, you're there in 15 minutes from most of central Geneva, you jump in, you drink a beer. That's it. Pure bliss.

The tip
  • Summer only — check it's open before making the trip
  • The beers are colder here than they have any right to be. Order one.
  • Plastic cups. I know. Trust the process.
  • Swim first, beer after — that's the correct order
  • River swimming is cleaner than the lake — fast-moving water, no duck fleas
  • Jonction further downstream is too crowded in peak summer. La Barge is the move.
