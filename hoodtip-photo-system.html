HoodTip Photo Layout System

Reference guide and copy-paste CSS for all tip pages.

The philosophy

Photos should enhance the writing, not just sit between paragraphs. The layout system has two modes: editorial blocks (full-bleed images that punctuate the article) and magazine floats (images that sit beside text so reading and looking happen simultaneously).

The main fix over the old system: portrait photos now use aspect-ratio: 3/4 instead of a fixed pixel height. This means they display at their natural proportions and don't look awkwardly cropped.

1. Full-bleed landscape — .img-full

Use for hero-quality single images that deserve the full width. The image breaks out of the article column and spans the full viewport.

<div class="img-full">
  <img src="photo.jpg" alt="Description" style="object-position: center 40%;" />
  <div class="img-caption">Optional caption text</div>
</div>

2. Landscape pair — .img-pair

Two landscape photos side by side, full bleed. Use 4/3 aspect ratio (enforced). Good for before/after, two angles of the same scene, interior + exterior.

<div class="img-pair">
  <img src="photo-a.jpg" alt="..." />
  <img src="photo-b.jpg" alt="..." />
</div>

3. Portrait pair — .img-portrait-pair

Two portrait (phone) photos side by side at natural 3/4 ratio. This is the main fix — no more forced heights that distort vertical photos.

<div class="img-portrait-pair">
  <img src="portrait-a.jpg" alt="..." />
  <img src="portrait-b.jpg" alt="..." />
</div>

4. Portrait single — .img-portrait-single

One portrait photo, centred in the article column at natural ratio. Max-width 460px. Use when a single vertical photo deserves its own moment.

<div class="img-portrait-single">
  <img src="portrait.jpg" alt="..." />
  <div class="img-caption">Caption</div>
</div>

5. Trio — .img-trio

Three landscape photos across full bleed. Good for neighbourhood/atmosphere shots where none individually deserves a hero treatment.

<div class="img-trio">
  <img src="a.jpg" alt="..." />
  <img src="b.jpg" alt="..." />
  <img src="c.jpg" alt="..." />
</div>

6 & 7. Magazine floats — .img-float-right / .img-float-left

The magazine treatment. The image floats beside the text so reading and looking happen at the same time. Add class portrait or landscape to set the aspect ratio. Always close with .cf (clearfix) on the containing element.

<!-- Float right with portrait photo -->
<div class="cf">
  <figure class="img-float-right portrait">
    <img src="portrait.jpg" alt="..." />
    <figcaption class="img-caption">Caption</figcaption>
  </figure>
  <p>Text wraps around the image. Write two or three
  paragraphs here — enough for the eye to travel between
  the image and the words naturally.</p>
  <p>Second paragraph. The float continues until you run
  out of text or hit the clearfix.</p>
</div>

<!-- Float left with landscape photo -->
<div class="cf">
  <figure class="img-float-left landscape">
    <img src="landscape.jpg" alt="..." />
  </figure>
  <p>Text to the right of the image.</p>
</div>

Recommended sequences for a full tip page

Restaurant/food tip: hero landscape → 2 paragraphs → float-right portrait → 1 paragraph → pull quote → full-bleed detail shot → portrait pair → tip box

Hotel/experience tip: hero landscape → 1 paragraph → float-left portrait (room/detail) → 1–2 paragraphs → full-bleed view shot → landscape pair (exterior + interior) → tip box

Short tip (coffee, bar, quick find): hero → 1 paragraph → portrait single or float → pull quote → tip box. Keep it tight.

Rules

— Never use a fixed pixel height (height: 580px) on portrait images — always use aspect-ratio
— Always add object-position inline to control crop (e.g. style="object-position: center 30%")
— Float sections need at least 2 paragraphs of text alongside — otherwise just use a portrait-single
— On mobile, floats collapse to full width automatically
— Captions are optional but add character — keep them to one line, sentence case
