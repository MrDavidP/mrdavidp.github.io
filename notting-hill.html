Notting Hillwest london proper

Portobello on a Saturday, the Electric on a rainy evening, a pint at The Cow any time. One of the neighbourhoods that earns the hype.

Getting there
Notting Hill Gate or Ladbroke Grove tube. Walk from Westbourne Park for Portobello
Go when
Saturday for the market. Any evening for dinner or the cinema. Rainy afternoons are made for The Cow
Best for
A genuinely great cinema, proper pub culture, oysters, and the kind of streets worth getting lost in

Notting Hill has a reputation problem — too famous, too pretty, too much the setting of a certain film — but spend a proper afternoon here and you'll forgive it all. Portobello is one of the best streets in London. The pubs are good. And the Electric Cinema is, genuinely, one of the finest ways to spend three hours on a rainy Tuesday in any city anywhere.

The Tips
01
Cinema
Electric Cinema

"Honestly the nicest cinema I've ever been to. It's part of Soho House but you don't need to be a member to watch a film — just buy a ticket. Get a glass of champagne or a little Negroni, order a snack, sink into a leather club chair, and enjoy whatever's on. The room itself — the ornate plasterwork ceiling, the red curtain, the little lamps on the armrests — is worth going for before the film even starts."

Pub
The Cow

"Best pint of Guinness in west London, maybe the city. The flow is right, the pour is right, the bar is properly old. Food is excellent — oysters, winkles, whelks, proper pub seafood done well. Sit at the bar. The dogs are good. This is what a pub is supposed to feel like."

The Electric — what to know
  • Book ahead — it fills up, especially weekends
  • They programme well. Check what's on before you go.
  • The bar is to the right as you enter. Order before you sit down.
  • Non-members can buy tickets online direct — no Soho House card needed
  • 191 Portobello Road, W11
The Cow — what to order
  • Guinness — obviously. Don't rush the pour.
  • Half a dozen rock oysters with a cold white
  • Winkles if they have them — underrated
  • Sit at the bar if you can get a stool
  • 89 Westbourne Park Road, W2
The pub dog situation

Dog-friendly, which tells you everything you need to know about a pub's intentions.
