Mulligans Irish Pub

9 people tipped this

Geneva's best pint of Guinness. Creamy, well-poured, the real thing. Get a bag of salt and vinegar crisps with it. Inside is classic cosy Irish pub — dark wood, sport on the telly, the kind of place that's the same in every city and somehow always right.

Outside is something Geneva doesn't usually offer — actual urban energy. It sits across from Manor, the big department store, on a street with enough foot traffic that you feel like you're in a proper city for an hour. Good people watching. Dogs welcome, apparently.

If you need to grab groceries on the way home, Manor is right there — so the cheeky pint pays for itself in efficiency.

"Outside feels like a proper city for an hour. Which in Geneva is rarer than you'd think."
