Carouge Market, Geneva

Paul-Henri Soler

Wine
11 people tipped this

The reason to go to Carouge market. Paul-Henri is a natural wine vigneron who sets up at the Saturday market with his bottles chalked up on a barrel and free tastings for anyone who stops. Local grapes, honest prices, the kind of wine you carry home carefully.

Get there before noon. Buy at least two bottles. The white is exceptional.
