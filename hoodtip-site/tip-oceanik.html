Hôtel Les Rives Oceanik

9 people tipped this

Not trying to be the best hotel in Paris, just a good one at a fair price in the right location. Around €200 a night for the 5th, which is honest money. Rooms are small but right — ironwork balcony, Haussmann view, Tintin poster on the wall.

Skip the hotel breakfast. Walk five minutes to Jardin du Luxembourg, find a café on the way, get a coffee and a pain au chocolat, do a lap of the gardens. That's your morning sorted.

The Latin Quarter earns its reputation — narrow streets, the kind of Paris that still feels like Paris. The hotel puts you right in it without charging Île Saint-Louis prices to do so.
