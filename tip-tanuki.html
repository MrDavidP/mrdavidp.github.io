Tanuki

8 people tipped this

Takeout only, and that's the whole point. An elderly Japanese couple running a tiny sushi spot on Rue Vignier — he makes the sushi, she handles everything else. No tables, no dine-in, no fuss. Just some of the freshest fish in Geneva out of a small kitchen.

The wasabi is homemade and excellent. When they're out of something, that's it for the day — so don't leave it too late. The large platter in the photo ran about 110 CHF, which is extremely fair considering any sit-down meal in Geneva will probably cost the same and not be half as good.

The move: get the large platter, stop at Uchitomi — the Japanese grocery nearby — for a good bottle of sake, go home. You've just made one of the best meals available in this city without setting foot in a restaurant.

"Get the large platter. Stop at Uchitomi for sake on the way home. Better than any restaurant and about the same price."
