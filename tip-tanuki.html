Tanuki

An elderly Japanese couple running a takeout sushi operation right off the main square. Some of the best sushi in Geneva. No tables, no fuss.

Address Rue Vignier 5
1205 Geneva
Hours
Tue–Fri11:30–15:00, 17:00–20:00 Saturday16:30–20:00 Sun–MonClosed
Phone 022 328 02 64
Best for Large platter, dinner at home
Tip by @dave

Right off the main square in Plainpalais. The husband makes the sushi to order behind a glass counter — when he's out of something (eel, for example), that's it for the day. The wife handles orders and the cash. Takeout only, no tables, no reservations. Just turn up.

Genuinely some of the best sushi in Geneva. The wasabi is homemade, which makes a larger difference than you'd expect — completely different from the stuff in little plastic sachets. The fish is good. The rice is good. The whole thing is quiet and honest.

"Get the large platter, grab sake from Uchitomi around the corner, go home, put on some cheesy Japanese restaurant music. That's the move."

Rue Vignier 5. The handwritten signs in the window are half the charm.

The platter in the photos cost about CHF 110 for the large — the Sushi MATSU. Honest money for Geneva, and guaranteed better than most meals that could easily end up costing more. They also do smaller boxes starting at CHF 19 if you're eating solo. The whole menu is posted in the window; worth a look before you go in.

On the way out: Uchitomi is a Japanese grocery a short walk away. Pick up sake cold — or grab one of their junmai ginjo bottles if they have it. Cold sake, the platter, candles if you can be bothered. It becomes a proper evening.

The large platter. Sushi MATSU — CHF 110. Worth every franc.

One thing to know: they sell out. Don't leave it too late in the evening, especially on a Friday. The second service starts at 17:00 — if you want the full selection, go early in that window. The eel and the sea urchin (oursin, CHF 9 a piece) go first.

Plated at home. Sake cold. This is what Friday evening looks like.
The menu
The full menu is posted in the window. Platters from CHF 19. Nigiri from CHF 3 per piece. Also: takoyaki (octopus balls) — 8pcs for CHF 12.
The tip
  • Get the large platter — the Sushi MATSU at CHF 110. Don't overthink it.
  • The wasabi is homemade. Use it. It's excellent and nothing like the tube stuff.
  • They sell out — don't go after 19:00 expecting the full menu. Go early in the 17:00 service.
  • The oursin (sea urchin) at CHF 9/piece is worth it if they have it.
  • Stop at Uchitomi — the Japanese grocery nearby — for sake on the way home.
  • Takeout only. No tables, no reservations. Just turn up.
  • Closed Sunday and Monday.
  • Tel: 022 328 02 64
