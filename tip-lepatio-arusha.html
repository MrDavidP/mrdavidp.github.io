Bar · Restaurant · Arusha, Tanzania

Get a beer.
Watch the football.

Le Patio is the right place to be in Arusha on a match night. Garden bar, open air, cold Kilimanjaro, big screen.

Tip by Dave
·
Le Patio
·
MJ9F+VF, Arusha, Tanzania

Arusha is a gateway city — most people pass through on the way to Kilimanjaro or the Serengeti. Le Patio is a reason to actually stop for the evening. Open-air garden bar, thatched roof, good cold beer, football on the big screen. The crowd is local and expat mixed, which is always the right sign.

Get a Kilimanjaro. Find a seat. Stay for the match.

The garden. Fire going. Right atmosphere.
