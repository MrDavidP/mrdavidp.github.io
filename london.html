London
Still one of the world's great cities.
CountryEngland
Neighbourhoods2
Tips4
LanguageEnglish

London is inexhaustible and slightly overwhelming, which is why neighbourhood-level guidance matters more here than almost anywhere. The city rewards specificity — knowing the right pub on the right street is the difference between a great evening and a forgettable one.

These tips are concentrated in West London, which is where I know it best. Notting Hill for a long weekend. Hampstead for a Sunday morning.
