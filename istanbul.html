Istanbul
The city is too big, the traffic is too bad. You need good tips or you will hate it here.
CountryTurkey
Neighbourhoods1
Tips5
LanguageTurkish

Istanbul rewards people who go beyond the obvious. The Bosphorus villages on the European side — Yeniköy, Bebek, Arnavutköy — are where you find the city at its most itself. Long meals, cold rakı, the water just there.

These tips are concentrated in Yeniköy, which is the right place to anchor yourself for an afternoon or an evening if you don't know where to start.
