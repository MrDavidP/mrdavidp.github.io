Domaine Saint-Amour
Honour system rosé in a UNESCO vineyard. Leave cash, take a bottle, sit on the patio.
Region Lavaux, Vaud
Getting there Train from Geneva — no car needed
Best for Sunday afternoon, picnic, cold rosé
Tip by @dave

Lavaux is one of the most beautiful wine regions in the world — UNESCO-listed, steep terraced vineyards dropping straight down to Lake Geneva with the French Alps across the water. Really one of the most beautiful places I've ever seen. You can just go and walk around, but in the spirit of HoodTip, here is a destination.

Domaine Saint-Amour. I went on a Sunday and I think it's closed on Sundays — but that's actually better, because it turns into the honour system. There's a little shack with a fridge full of cold rosé made from the grapes surrounding you. Bring cash. Leave it in the box. Take a bottle. Then go downstairs and sit on their patio overlooking the vineyards and have a picnic.

"Leave cash in the box. Take a bottle. Sit on the patio with the lake below you and the Alps across the water. An A+ way to spend an afternoon."

Lavaux. UNESCO. The Alps across the water.

Not sure exactly how it works when they're open — probably pretty similar, you just hand the cash to a human instead of the honour system box. Either way, this is a good place to aim for if you want to visit Lavaux but don't know exactly where to go. The train drops you nearby so no car needed. Turn up, find the fridge, sit in the vines.

The tip
  • Take the train from Geneva — no car needed, the train drops you nearby
  • Bring cash for the honour system. The fridge is full of cold rosé made from the vines around you.
  • Sunday is honour-system-only — which is honestly the better version
  • Go downstairs to the patio after. Bring food. Sit in the vineyard. Stay as long as you want.
  • One of the most beautiful places in Switzerland. Just go.
