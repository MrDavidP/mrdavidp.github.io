Domaine Saint-Amour

Wine
6 people tipped this

Lavaux is one of the most beautiful wine regions in the world — UNESCO-listed, steep terraced vineyards dropping down to Lake Geneva with the French Alps across the water. The problem is knowing where to go.

Go here. A little hut in the vines with a fridge full of cold rosé they grow on site. Honour system — leave cash in the box, take a bottle, sit down in the vineyard. The train drops you nearby so no car needed.

An A+ way to spend an afternoon.

Getting there
Take the train from Geneva or Lausanne to Cully or Épesses — both are a short walk from the domaine. No car needed, which means you can actually drink the rosé.
