Geneva
The city of refuge.
CountrySwitzerland
Neighbourhoods11
Tips49
LanguageFrench

Home to international organisations, banks, and watches — and the people behind them. The French Alps in the distance, vineyards, a lake, two rivers. Switzerland's francophone outpost feels quietly utopian. A good place to raise kids.

The city is compact and walkable. Most of what's worth knowing sits within a few neighbourhoods. Start with Carouge on a Saturday morning.
