Manor Food Hall

The best grocery in central Geneva. Not even close.

Address Rue de Cornavin 6, 1201 Geneva
Neighbourhood Saint-Gervais
Best for Fresh fish, meat, local produce
Tip by @dave

Manor is a Swiss department store — shoes, watches, housewares, the lot — but the food hall in the basement is what you're here for. It's the best grocery in central Geneva by a significant margin, and the fishmonger is in a different league from anything else in the neighbourhood.

The fish comes from real places. There's a fishmonger based in Nyon who supplies them with fera, the local lake fish — white, mild, incredibly fresh — and it's one of those things you can only really get here. The sardine fillets, the salmon, the selection of cured meats from the Valais. The bread is good. The local vegetables section is properly curated, not just a sticker that says local on something from Spain.

Local. Produit de votre région. They mean it.

The thing to know about Manor is the daily sales — they call it action. Every day there's something significantly reduced in the fish or meat counter. Sometimes it's lamb chops, sometimes a good steak, sometimes scallops or salmon. Worth checking what's on action before you decide what you're cooking. Half the time it's better than whatever you planned.

"The fishmonger gets fera from Lake Geneva. That alone is worth the trip."

Filet de brochet. Pêcherie Deli, Nyon. CHF 6.95. Local means local.

And it's a department store — so if you need a pair of shoes, a gift, a watch, a kitchen knife, or something for the house, you're already there. Efficient Geneva errand running.

The tip
  • Go to the fishmonger first — ask what's fresh, get the fera if they have it
  • Check what's on action at the meat and fish counter before deciding what to cook
  • Viande séchée du Valais from the charcuterie counter — better than anything packaged
  • The local produce section is genuinely local — worth using over the supermarket alternatives
  • It's also a full department store — shoes, watches, homewares, presents — all in the same building
  • Food hall is in the basement. Follow the signs from the main entrance on Rue de Cornavin
