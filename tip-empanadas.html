Empanadas Factory

7 people tipped this

If you work near the UN you already know the problem — there is almost nothing good to eat in this neighbourhood unless you're a senior diplomat with two hours and an expense account. Empanadas Factory is one of the exceptions.

Grab empanadas on the way out if you're in a rush. But if you have 25 francs and want to sit down and eat something properly, get the Beef Boliviano — steak and egg on rice with fries, the whole thing. Décor is full Saved by the Bell. It works.

The real move is the sauce picante — a little orange salsa they make in house. You have to ask for it. Don't forget to ask for it.

"Ask for the sauce picante. They make it in house. You have to ask for it. Don't forget to ask for it."
